## Sequence identity vs embedding cosine

Sampling uses two strata: uniform random pairs, plus embedding nearest-neighbor pairs added purely to populate the high-similarity region (they are enriched for high cosine **by construction** and are never pooled into correlation statistics).

- random stratum: 2000 pairs, Pearson r = 0.084
- low-identity (<20%) / high-cosine (>0.9): 571 random, 1215 in the NN-enriched stratum (of 1976 NN pairs)
- high-identity (>50%) / low-cosine (<0.7): 0 random, 0 NN

High-cosine low-identity pairs are *representation-space neighbors*; no claim of biological convergence is made without evidence.
